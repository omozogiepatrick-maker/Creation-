Case Studies

The bottleneck. The solution. The outcome.

Every project starts with a real operational problem — not a request for "a website." Here's how three of them played out.

CASE 01 · SAAS

SaaS platform redesign

The Challenge

An early-stage SaaS company's dashboard had grown feature by feature for two years with no redesign. New users churned during onboarding, and the team couldn't say why.

The Bottleneck Discovered

Onboarding required 14 manual steps before a user saw any value. Most drop-off happened before step 5 — a UX problem, not a product problem.

The Solution

A rebuilt dashboard with a guided, self-serve setup flow that got users to their first result in under 3 steps.

Technology Used

Modern frontend framework, redesigned information architecture, self-serve onboarding logic.

The Outcome

Page load times dropped significantly and onboarding became fully self-serve for the first time — no support ticket required to get started.

Lesson learned: the real bottleneck was rarely the feature set — it was the number of decisions a new user had to make before feeling any progress.

CASE 02 · E-COMMERCE

E-commerce launch for a retail brand

The Challenge

A retail brand sold exclusively through in-person and social media DMs — orders, payments, and inventory were tracked manually across three different tools.

The Bottleneck Discovered

Every sale required manual reconciliation between what was sold, what was in stock, and what had actually been paid for — hours of admin work weekly, and regular stock errors.

The Solution

A full storefront with a connected product catalog, checkout, and payments — inventory updates automatically with every order.

Technology Used

E-commerce storefront, integrated payments, real-time inventory sync.

The Outcome

Launch-ready in weeks, with manual reconciliation work eliminated entirely — the owner got hours of their week back.

Lesson learned: the business didn't need "a website" — it needed one system instead of three disconnected ones.

CASE 03 · STARTUP

Startup landing page & MVP site

The Challenge

A pre-launch startup needed to validate demand and start collecting early users before the product itself was fully built.

The Bottleneck Discovered

Without a credible landing page, cold outreach and early conversations weren't converting — people needed something to look at before they'd commit.

The Solution

A conversion-focused landing page paired with a lightweight functional MVP for the first cohort of early users.

Technology Used

Landing page, lightweight web app, early-access signup flow.

The Outcome

The founder had something credible to send in outreach within days, not months — and started collecting real user feedback immediately.

Lesson learned: early-stage credibility often matters more than early-stage feature completeness.
